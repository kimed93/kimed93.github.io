I am **Eden Kim**, an AI researcher at the **Energy Intelligence Research Laboratory, Electronics and Telecommunications Research Institute (ETRI)**, Republic of Korea.

My research focuses on developing **predictive, robust, and generalizable AI methodologies** for intelligent condition monitoring, prognostics and health management (PHM), and predictive maintenance in industrial and energy systems. In particular, I investigate learning-based approaches for **anomaly detection, fault diagnosis, degradation modeling, and remaining useful life (RUL) estimation** using complex sensor and time-series data.

From a methodological perspective, my research explores **predictive and self-supervised time-series representation learning**, with an emphasis on capturing temporal dependencies and underlying system dynamics. I am particularly interested in integrating **Koopman operator-based learning and latent dynamical modeling** into representation learning frameworks to develop informative, dynamics-aware, and generalizable representations.

My research also extends to **AI-driven energy applications and intelligent inspection technologies**, including **deep learning-based image deblurring for wind turbine blade inspection**. This work investigates image restoration approaches to improve visual information acquired under challenging inspection conditions, supporting more reliable monitoring and inspection of energy infrastructure.

Furthermore, I am interested in **generalizable and adaptive machine learning**, including cross-domain learning, active learning, and data-efficient adaptation, to address the challenges of heterogeneous operating conditions and distribution shifts in real-world industrial environments.

My long-term research goal is to advance **reliable and transferable AI frameworks** that integrate intelligent sensing, representation learning, and predictive modeling for industrial prognostics and energy system monitoring.

## Research Interests

### Prognostics, Health Management & Predictive Maintenance
- Anomaly detection and intelligent fault diagnosis
- Remaining useful life (RUL) estimation and degradation modeling
- Condition monitoring and predictive health assessment
- Data-driven predictive maintenance for industrial and energy systems

### Time-Series Representation Learning
- Predictive and self-supervised representation learning
- Temporal dependency modeling and latent dynamics learning
- Koopman operator-based learning and dynamical systems modeling
- Dynamics-aware and generalizable representation learning

### Energy AI and Intelligent Monitoring
- AI-driven monitoring and inspection of energy infrastructure
- Wind turbine blade image deblurring and restoration
- Deep learning-based visual inspection and condition assessment

### Generalizable and Adaptive Machine Learning
- Cross-domain and cross-dataset generalization
- Active learning and domain adaptation
- Data-efficient and robust learning under distribution shifts
